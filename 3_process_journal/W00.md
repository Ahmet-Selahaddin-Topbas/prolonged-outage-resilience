## B. The week's record

### 1. What happened
- **25 Oct.** First framing: compare decision methods and social acceptance for energy choices. A literature search found a closely related study judging the approaches "generally consistent".
- **26 to 29 Oct.** The question was changed to one with a practical answer: *which energy source holds when the grid does not, and for how long.* Hydrogen is one of four candidates, tested rather than assumed. Context: Adana is one of the ten provinces in the 2023 earthquake state-of-emergency decision (D07), which makes a prolonged-outage question concrete for the campus.
- **30 Oct.** Literature scan: ten Scopus queries plus web searches, about thirty works screened, six read in relevant sections. Scope locked as v2.0. Originality claim fixed as a *limited-search* statement. First predictions written (`pre_hypothesis/v1.0`).
- **4 Nov.** Project title fixed: *Energy Resilience Under Prolonged Grid Outages: Which Source Holds When the Grid Doesn't?* Alternatives containing "optimisation" or "techno-economic" were rejected as overclaiming. Meeting with an ATÜ faculty member (name withheld): interest in the project and in the Japan goal; first task is a campus site record and diagram using synthetic data; advice to carry out the modelling with Claude. A funding call (TÜBİTAK 2209-A) was mentioned; any application would be handled by the faculty member. Literature and source register compiled for the meeting.
- **5 to 6 Nov.** Model skeleton M01 (v0.0, then v0.1 with three tiers). Campus site field form and a diagram prepared from Google Maps names (unverified).
- **7 Nov.** Model M02 v0.2 with a site configuration and 29 self-tests. This repository created. The earlier Léargas SI repository moved under the same account so that the work has one public identity; whitepaper 003 (document dated May 2026) added to it.
- **Contact log.** 21 Oct: reply from the UTSIP office (2027 details not final; one session; lab list expected around the end of November), D03. 4 Nov: meeting with the ATÜ faculty member above.
