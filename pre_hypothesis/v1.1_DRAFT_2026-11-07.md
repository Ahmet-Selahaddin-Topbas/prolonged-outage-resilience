# Researcher Pre-Hypothesis v1.1 (DRAFT, not yet frozen)
*Draft of 7 Nov 2026. Becomes binding only when the author approves and the repository commit "pre-hypothesis v1.1 frozen" is made. v1.0 (30 Oct 2026) stays in this folder unchanged. After freezing, changes are added as dated amendments at the end, never as silent edits.*

## Why a v1.1
v1.0 was written before any model existed. While building the model skeleton, three of the six statements turned out not to be testable as written. They are sharpened here. **Disclosure:** the skeleton (M01, 4 and 6 Nov; M02, 7 Nov) was run with *placeholder* inputs and its figures were seen by the author before this revision. Those figures are not results. They revealed *how to state a prediction so it can be tested*, for example that all options tie at 100 % for short durations. Threshold values below are justified by the stated rationale, but the author had already seen the placeholder curves, so they were set with that knowledge; read them with that in mind. No prediction was changed to make a placeholder curve agree with it.

## Fixed definitions (apply to every prediction)
- **Equal stored energy:** all options start with the same stored electrical-equivalent energy, all stores full. Predictions are tested at several values of it (2,000, 5,000, 10,000 and 20,000 kWh), not at one chosen size.
- **Survival share at duration d:** fraction of outage start hours at which the chosen service level is served completely for d hours (equal weight, not a probability of a real outage).
- **Short** = d ≤ 24 h. **Long** = d ≥ 168 h. **Ahead** = strictly higher survival share (ties do not count as ahead).
- **Options:** Generator (no resupply), Battery only, Solar + battery, Solar + hydrogen. The generator-with-resupply variant is analysed separately.
- **Service levels:** K1 only, K1+K2, K1+K2+K3. **Conditions:** Base, Heat wave (+8 °C, outages starting in the hottest 7 days).
- When real data replace placeholders, the predictions are tested again with the real inputs.

## Predictions
| # | Prediction | Basis | Would be wrong if |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Serving K1+K2 in the base condition: (a) at short durations the generator is not worse than any other option; (b) its survival share falls from 0.9 to 0.1 within a band of at most 48 h of duration. | Fuel autonomy is a hard limit; load repeats daily, so start-hour differences span about two daily cycles. | Any option is better at a short duration, or the band is wider than 48 h. |
| P2 | Battery alone, serving K1 only: mean autonomy for outages starting in the hottest 30 days is at least 15 % shorter than for the coolest 30 days. | Cooling load in Adana summers raises the tier-1 load. | The reduction is smaller than 15 %. |
| P3 | Serving K1+K2+K3 with the electrolyser sized to store daytime solar surplus: the shortest duration at which Solar + hydrogen is ahead of Solar + battery is at least 72 h in the base condition, and later (or never) in the heat-wave condition. | Round-trip losses of the hydrogen chain; extra cooling load in heat waves. | It is ahead below 72 h, or not later in the heat wave. |
| P4 | For both solar-based options serving K1+K2, the survival share at 168 h for outages starting between 18:00 and 05:59 is at least 0.01 lower than for starts between 06:00 and 17:59. | No solar input until morning. | The difference is smaller than 0.01 for either option. |
| P5 | No option from the four has zero worst-case regret in all six combinations of service level and condition (durations 24, 72, 168, 336 h). | Each source has a different limit. | One option has zero worst-case regret in all six. |
| P6 | Water for electrolysis is not limiting: over a 14-day outage the water need (mean and 95th percentile over start hours) is at most 10 % of the usable volume of the campus main water tank. | The need is physical (about 9 kg water per kg hydrogen); the open question is supply. | The need exceeds 10 %. **Status: open until the tank volume is obtained (data item to be added).** |

## What would change my mind (unchanged from v1.0)
- A result in which hydrogen is never ahead under any tested condition: reported as a valid finding.
- Evidence that real campus load differs strongly from the synthetic profile: results recomputed with the real load and the change documented.
- A published work offering the full combination in the originality statement: the statement is revised and the revision logged.
